STEP 7 - Coordinate incident updates.
Prepare concise communications from verified evidence for the on-call engineer to review and send. Do not state a root cause you have not proven. Do not say the service has recovered unless the action record or recovery evidence given to you shows it.

Sections, in this order:
## Status
## Status update
Three lines or fewer for stakeholders: what is happening, impact, next update time.
Evidence: ids.
## Impact summary
Who and what is affected, how much, since when. Evidence: ids.
## Handoff note
For the next on-call engineer: current state, what was tried, what is pending, what to watch, open hypotheses (as hypotheses). Evidence: ids.
