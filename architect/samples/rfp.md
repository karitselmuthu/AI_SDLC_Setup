# RFP: Customer Support Portal - Acme Retail
Problem: support staff juggle 3 ticket tools; customers wait 2 days for answers.
Objectives: single portal, reduce first response time to under 4 hours.
Scope: web portal, ticket import from legacy tools, SSO. Mobile app is out of scope.
Submission deadline: 30 June. Go-live: 1 October.
Evaluation: price 40%, technical approach 40%, references 20%.
Requirements:
- The portal must support SSO via the customer's identity provider.
- Tickets from the 3 legacy tools must be imported with full history.
- The portal should offer a customer-facing knowledge base search.
- Chat support may be added in a later phase.
- The system must be available 99.9% of the time during business hours.
- Agents should be able to see all of a customer's tickets in one view.
