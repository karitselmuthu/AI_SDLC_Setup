Task: PREPARE THE PROPOSAL PACKAGE.

Draft a coherent proposal package for review, using the opportunity brief, requirements, current-state view,
options, target architecture, enterprise-fit assessment, delivery approach and ROM estimate listed below, plus
the bidder's own documents (profile, credentials, case studies, references) if supplied.

Rules:
- Tailor the language to the prospect: use their name, their stated problem and objectives, and their own words
  (e.g. the RFP's terms). Organise the argument around their evaluation criteria and weights.
- Every factual claim about the bidder (experience, results, certifications, partnerships, uptime, speed, references)
  is either EVIDENCED, with the source file named in the evidence register, or marked inline "[CONFIRM: what and who]".
  Never invent credentials, customers, outcomes or numbers. Marketing statements without evidence are [CONFIRM], not facts.
  Do not claim a certification the documents say is not held. Do not use a reference whose approval is pending
  without [CONFIRM: reference approval].
- No unsupported superlatives ("industry-leading", "best-in-class", "fastest"). Prefer a specific, evidenced fact.
- Do not promise what the earlier analysis says cannot be met. Where a deadline or requirement is not achievable,
  conditional, or an exception, say so honestly in client-appropriate words and state the condition.
  A requirement the design marks Not met or Unknown must never be answered "Full".
- Client-facing sections must not contain internal-only material: the bidder's internal rate card, cost or margin,
  pricing strategy, assumptions about competitors or how the client will score, or internal risk commentary.
  Pricing: use a figure only if a document says it is approved for release; otherwise write "[CONFIRM: pricing approval]"
  and describe only the structure (what is included, how it is charged). ROM estimates are internal and are never
  presented as a price.
- Use the term "Proposed" for anything the client has not stated or agreed. Keep assumptions visible.
- Diagrams are Mermaid, drawn only from the target architecture and delivery approach.

Reply in markdown with exactly these sections, in this order:
